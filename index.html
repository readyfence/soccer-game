Hold a kick button to charge power, release to kick. Bicycle kicks (B / LT+Y) only connect when the ball is in the air. Sprint uses a stamina bar — when it empties you can only jog until it fills, so pass to a fresh teammate. Left stick aims; right stick adds curve. Penalties: aim the dive with the stick (or WASD) and press J / X to dive left, right, or stay. Penalty takers get a stress meter — big moments, weaker finishers and hesitating all raise it, making the aim ring wobble and shrinking the white power sweet spot (over it skies, under it scuffs).
